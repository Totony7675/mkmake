Make learning to write an experience! Premium, solid wood practice board on which your child can learn to write all letters of the Hungarian alphabet. Playful learning, solid foundations.

This board was specifically designed to accompany the learning process. The board is DOUBLE-SIDED and contains the complete Hungarian alphabet, all 44 letters, including double and triple letters (Cs, Dz, Dzs, Gy, Ly, Ny, Sz, Ty, Zs):

- Side A: Cursive capital letters – the complete alphabet in cursive.
- Side B: Cursive lowercase letters – the complete alphabet in cursive.

What makes this board different?

- Deep grooves: The letters are not laser-burned but CNC-milled. The deeper grooves excellently guide the included wooden pencil, helping to establish correct movements and muscle memory.
- The whole alphabet on one board: The larger board fits the complete alphabet, so the grooves are finer, and the letter stroke guidance is precisely traceable.
- Solid quality: Made of solid beech. Stable, durable piece, not a thin veneered board.
- Natural finish: Treated with a homemade beeswax and mineral oil blend, giving the wood a silky feel and protection, without chemicals.
- Accessory: Every board comes with a hand-turned wooden "pencil" made by me. This Montessori-inspired tool excellently develops fine motor skills and hand-eye coordination.

This board is not personalized; it contains the complete Hungarian alphabet. Each piece is made by hand, so slight variations in appearance may occur.
