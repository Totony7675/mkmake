Make the first steps of learning to write a joy! Premium, personalized wooden practice board with which your child will easily learn to write their name. Playful learning, solid foundations.

This board was specifically designed to accompany the learning process. Unlike most boards on the market, this product is DOUBLE-SIDED:

- Side A: Printed capital letters – Perfect for starting out, for learning letter shapes.
- Side B: Official Hungarian school cursive – The standard cursive font taught in schools.

What makes this board different?

- Deep grooves: The letters are not laser-burned but CNC-milled. The deeper grooves excellently guide the included wooden pencil, helping to establish correct movements and muscle memory.
- Solid quality: Made of solid beech, more than 2 cm thick. Stable, durable piece, not a thin veneered board.
- Natural finish: Treated with a homemade beeswax and mineral oil blend, giving the wood a silky feel and protection, without chemicals.
- Accessory: Every board comes with a wooden "pencil". This Montessori-inspired tool excellently develops fine motor skills and hand-eye coordination.
